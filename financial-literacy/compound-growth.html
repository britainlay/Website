Compound Growth.

Understanding how time, consistency, and growth can work together over the long term.

01 — THE BASICS

What is compound growth?

Compound growth describes a process where growth can build on previous growth over time.

A simple way to think about it is that your money can potentially grow, and then future growth can build on what came before it. The longer the time period, the more opportunities there may be for this process to continue.

Growth + Time = Potential The longer the period, the more compounding can potentially matter.
02 — THE POWER OF TIME

05 — MY PERSPECTIVE

What compound growth taught me.

I first learned about compound growth through investing. I was honestly appalled by how much compounding can potentially do over time and how different the numbers can become when you look at a longer period. Seeing hypothetical numbers grow made the concept feel much more real to me. It showed me why starting early can give you an advantage.

Start as soon as possible.

To me, compound growth is all about the long-term. It's not about trying to become wealthy overnight. It's about understanding how time and consistent financial decisions can potentially make a difference.

06 — KEEP IT REAL

Compounding isn't magic.

Compound growth can be powerful, but there are also things that can work against long-term growth.

Waiting too long

Starting later can mean having less time for potential growth to build.

Inconsistency

Building financial habits often requires consistency rather than relying on one-time decisions.

Fees and costs

Fees and other costs can reduce the amount of money that remains invested and available for potential growth.

Expecting guarantees

Investment returns are not guaranteed. Markets can rise and fall, so hypothetical examples should never be treated as promises.
