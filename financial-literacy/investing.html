Investing.

Understanding risk, research, and long-term growth — and how my own experience has shaped the way I think about investing.

01 — THE BASICS

What is investing?

Investing means putting money into an asset with the expectation that it may increase in value or generate income over time. Unlike saving, investing involves risk, and returns are never guaranteed.

Stocks

A stock represents ownership in a company. Investors may benefit if the company's value increases, but the investment can also lose value.

Bonds

Bonds generally represent lending money to an issuer in exchange for interest payments and repayment under specified terms.

Funds

Funds can hold collections of investments, allowing investors to gain exposure to multiple assets through one investment.

02 — BUILDING A FOUNDATION

Think before you invest.

For me, investing is not just about choosing an investment. Understanding risk, diversification, and your time horizon can help create a stronger foundation for making decisions.

01

Risk & Reward

Investments can potentially grow in value or generate income, but greater potential returns can also involve greater uncertainty. Investments can lose value.

02

Diversification

Diversification means spreading investments across different assets rather than relying entirely on one investment.

03

Long-Term Thinking

Investing can involve looking beyond short-term price movements and thinking about goals, time horizons, risk, and strategy.

06 — FOR BEGINNERS

Learn before you leap.

If you're interested in investing, I think the best place to start is by learning the basics. There are many resources online, including YouTube, that can help you understand how the stock market works. I also recommend using a simulator to practice and explore different investments without putting real money at risk. From there, explore the stock market, research companies, and continue learning as you gain experience.
